Welcome to **4 in a Row**! Drop your tokens and line up four before your opponent.

#### Features

- 🤖 **Against the iPhone** — Four levels, from Easy to Unbeatable
- 👥 **Two Players, One Phone** — Play face to face
- 🌍 **Online With Friends** — Invite a friend through Game Center
- 🎨 **Three Styles** — Toon, Pixel or Comic, in light or dark mode
- 🏆 **Stats and Leaderboards** — Wins, losses and draws by level, Game Center achievements
- 🚫 **Ads Never in Your Way** — Never during a game, removable with one purchase
