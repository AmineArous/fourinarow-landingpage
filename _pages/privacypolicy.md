# Privacy Policy

At **4 in a Row**, your privacy is our top priority.

<br>

## 1.0 What Stays on Your Device

The game in progress, your statistics and your settings (style, sound and vibrations) are stored only on your device.

<br>

## 2.0 Anonymous Usage Statistics

To understand how the game is played and improve it, 4 in a Row uses **Google Analytics for Firebase**. It records:

- When a game is started and how it ends (win, loss or draw), with the mode and the level  
- When an ad is shown  
- When the shop is opened, and purchases (product and price)  
- When the style, sound or vibrations are changed  
- Standard app events, such as app opens and sessions  

These events are tied to a random identifier generated for this installation of the App, not to your name, email or Apple Account. Google derives an approximate country or region from a masked IP address. These statistics do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Google's handling of this data is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

<br>

## 3.0 Advertising

4 in a Row is free and shows ads through **Google AdMob**: a banner outside the board, and a full-screen ad between some games. No ad is ever shown during a game or over the board. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

- Where the law requires it, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before any ad. You can change it at any time in the game's settings.  
- iOS then asks whether the App may track you. If you refuse, ads are shown without the advertising identifier. You can change this choice in the Settings app of your device.  
- Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking), your IP address, your approximate location and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads.  

Google's handling of this data is described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

<br>

## 4.0 Game Center

Online games with friends, leaderboards and achievements use **Apple's Game Center**, only if you are signed in to it. Your Game Center nickname, scores and moves in online games are handled by Apple, as described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

<br>

## 5.0 What We Don't Do

- ❌ We don't collect your name, email or contacts  
- ❌ We don't track you across other apps or websites without your permission  
- ❌ We don't sell any data  
- ❌ We don't require an account  

<br>

## 6.0 In-App Purchases

Removing the ads, and some styles and token sets, are one-time purchases processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment information. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

<br>

## 7.0 Other Permissions

4 in a Row does not request access to your location, photos, contacts, calendar, camera or microphone.

<br>

## 8.0 Children's Privacy

4 in a Row does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The anonymous usage statistics described above are not tied to anyone's identity. The App contains ads, unless they have been removed.

<br>

## 9.0 Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
