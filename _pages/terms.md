## 2.0 Description of Service

4 in a Row is a four-in-a-row game, played against the iPhone, with a friend on the same device, or online with friends. The App uses:
- Local device storage to save your game in progress, statistics and settings  
- Apple's Game Center for online games, leaderboards and achievements  
- Google AdMob to show ads (see the [Privacy Policy](../privacypolicy/))  
- The App Store (StoreKit) for optional purchases  
- Google Analytics for Firebase, for anonymous usage statistics (see the [Privacy Policy](../privacypolicy/))
